# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 16, 2026

**JoltSync** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how JoltSync accesses, uses, and protects your information when you use our web application.

## 1. Information We Access
When you sign in with Google, JoltSync requests limited OAuth permissions (`drive.file`, `userinfo.email`, and `userinfo.profile`) to operate its core features:
- **Basic Profile Information:** Your name, email address, and profile picture, used solely to display your signed-in account in the App interface.
- **Selected Google Drive & Google Docs Files:** Access strictly limited to files created by JoltSync or explicitly opened/selected by you within the App. JoltSync cannot view or access unrelated files in your Google Drive.
- **Uploaded Photos & Writing Metrics:** Handwritten note images you upload for text extraction, text drafted inside the editor, and session productivity metrics (word count, drafting/editing duration, and words per hour).

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the accessed data exclusively to provide JoltSync's functionality:
- Extracting text from uploaded photos of handwritten notes and inserting it into your chosen Google Doc.
- Syncing your written text, formatting, and writing session metadata directly to your personal Google Drive account so it can be synced with your Jolt log.
- Displaying your active account and document status within the App.

## 3. Data Storage & Zero-Database Architecture
- **No External User Database:** JoltSync operates client-side and does **not** store your documents, handwritten images, or writing session logs on external servers.
- **Stored in Your Google Drive:** All documents and session sync files remain inside your own Google Drive account under your control.
- **Session Tokens:** OAuth access tokens are held temporarily in your browser session to authenticate requests directly with Google APIs and expire automatically.

## 4. Data Sharing & Google API Limited Use Disclosure
- We do **not** sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data or Google user data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
- JoltSync’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the **Google API Services User Data Policy**, including the **Limited Use** requirements.

## 5. Revoking Access & Data Deletion
- Because your files are stored directly in your Google Drive, you may view, edit, or delete them at any time in Google Drive.
- You can revoke JoltSync’s access to your Google Account at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions page (`https://myaccount.google.com/permissions`).

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
